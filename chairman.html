CHAIRMAN

Sri P. Achaiah Naidu

Sri P.Achaiah Naidu is the visionary leader behind Sri Venkateswara School of Nursing. With decades of experience in healthcare and education, he has dedicated his life to nurturing compassionate and skilled nursing professionals.

Under his guidance, the institution has grown into a center of excellence, combining academic rigor with practical training. His commitment to integrity, service, and empowerment continues to inspire both faculty and students.

Sri P.Achaiah Naidu believes that nursing is not just a profession, but a calling to serve humanity with compassion and dedication.
