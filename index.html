Our Institution

Sri Venkateswara School of Nursing is dedicated to providing comprehensive nursing education and training. We are recognized by the Government of Andhra Pradesh, ensuring quality standards and professional excellence.

Our commitment is to develop nursing professionals who are equipped with both theoretical knowledge and practical skills to provide compassionate patient care and contribute to the healthcare sector.

Our Mission

To impart quality nursing education that focuses on developing competent, ethical, and compassionate nursing professionals capable of meeting the healthcare needs of the community and nation.
