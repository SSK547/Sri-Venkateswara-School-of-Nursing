🎓 About Us

Sri Venkateswara School of Nursing is a distinguished institution dedicated to nurturing the next generation of healthcare professionals. Established with a vision to provide quality nursing education, the school has consistently upheld high standards of academic excellence, clinical training, and ethical practice.

We believe that nursing is not just a profession, but a calling rooted in compassion, service, and responsibility. Our institution strives to instill these values in every student, ensuring they graduate as competent, confident, and caring nurses ready to serve society.

🏛 Our Legacy

  • Founded on the principles of service and dedication to healthcare.
  • Guided by experienced faculty with a passion for teaching and mentoring.
  • A track record of producing graduates who excel in hospitals, community health centers, and global healthcare environments.

🤝 Our Commitment

  • To deliver education that blends theory with practical exposure.
  • To foster innovation and adaptability in the ever‑evolving medical field.
  • To encourage students to actively participate in community health programs, awareness campaigns, and outreach services.

⭐ Vision

To be recognized as a premier nursing institution that develops competent professionals dedicated to holistic patient care and community well‑being.

🎯 Mission

  • Deliver comprehensive nursing education aligned with national and global standards.
  • Instill values of empathy, integrity, and ethical practice.
  • Empower students with clinical expertise and leadership skills.
  • Promote community health initiatives and lifelong learning.

💙 Why Choose Us?

  • Experienced and dedicated faculty.
  • Balanced focus on academics, clinical practice, and values.
  • Strong emphasis on community service and patient‑centered care.
  • Supportive environment fostering confidence and professionalism.
